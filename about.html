About MNL Tyres

A customer-focused approach to tyres, vehicle care and helpful service.

Our Story

MNL Tyres is a tyre-business concept developed around a simple goal: helping motorists find tyres and services suited to their vehicles and driving needs.

Our proposed range includes tyres for passenger cars, SUVs, 4x4s and commercial vehicles. The website also introduces tyre fitting, wheel balancing, wheel alignment and tyre-repair services.

We aim to make finding information straightforward, from exploring tyre categories to requesting a quote.

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To help motorists make informed tyre choices through clear information, helpful communication and customer-focused service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted choice for motorists seeking dependable tyre products and services.

Why Choose Us?

Our approach is guided by the following priorities:

  • Customer focus: Understanding each customer's vehicle requirements and service needs.
  • Clear communication: Providing understandable information about tyre options and available services.
  • Convenience: Making it easy to browse products, view services and submit an enquiry online.
  • Responsible service: Encouraging attention to tyre condition and regular vehicle maintenance.
